Data & methodology

Where the numbers come from

MarketLab only shows what its sources say, labels estimates and delays, and never lets a test use information that wasn't known at the time.

Prices

Yahoo Finance daily bars (adjusted for splits and dividends) and delayed quotes. Intraday futures: a composite of Yahoo contract series, the continuous front month and the FirstRate sample — each candle keeps its source. Bad bars are flagged, never silently "fixed".

Company facts

SEC EDGAR filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, XBRL financials) first; Yahoo Finance fills gaps and provides analyst estimates, always marked as estimates.

Dependencies & exposures

Read from each company's latest annual report: suppliers, customers, geographies, policy topics and theme language, with the quoted sentence. Nothing is inferred from general knowledge.

Correlation ≠ dependency

Price correlation is measured from daily returns. Fundamental dependency comes from filings. Macro sensitivity is measured against market proxies (rates, dollar, oil, Nasdaq). They are shown side by side and never substituted for each other.

No look-ahead

Every condition decides a bar using only data available at that bar's close. Earnings count from the first session that could react. Intraday concepts are known only when confirmed.

No silent optimisation

MarketLab never tunes a strategy until it looks profitable. Every variant it tests is counted, and every relaxed rule is shown as original → relaxed.

Research, not advice. MarketLab doesn't recommend buying or selling anything.
